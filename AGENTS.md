# KLIK I.T. Project Instructions

These instructions apply to the entire KLIK I.T. website repository.

## Project and brand

- Business name: KLIK I.T.
- Descriptor: WEB DESIGN & DIGITAL SOLUTIONS
- Tagline: Good ideas deserve a place on the internet.
- Business location and audience: South African small businesses and individuals across age groups and sectors.
- Main service categories:
  1. Website Design & Development
  2. Professional & Personal Branding
  3. Digital Business Solutions
- The first milestone is the homepage only. Do not add other pages unless asked.

## Brand colours

- Electric cobalt: `#3155E7`
- Clean white: `#FFFFFF`
- Soft black: `#202124`
- Soft periwinkle: `#EEF1FF`

Use these colours consistently. Maintain sufficient text and interface contrast; do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning.

## Logo and assets

- The approved logo will be supplied as an image asset.
- Preserve the supplied logo file unchanged and retain its aspect ratio wherever it is displayed. Do not redraw, recolour, crop, distort, or otherwise modify it.
- Keep approved image assets in `public/images/` unless the project structure is deliberately revised.
- Do not invent client work, testimonials, customer names, or business statistics. Clearly label any portfolio examples as demonstrations or concepts.

## Design principles

- Aim for a modern, clean, professional site with bold editorial typography and generous whitespace.
- Use clean white backgrounds, electric cobalt accents, soft black text, and soft periwinkle sparingly.
- Keep page layouts uncluttered and easy to understand for different age groups and business sectors.
- Avoid excessive gradients, playful arts-and-crafts styling, unnecessary animation, clutter, and generic AI-generated website patterns.
- Prefer simple, maintainable HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Do not add dependencies without a clear need.
- Explain the purpose of new files and avoid creating unnecessary complexity.
- Ask before making major architectural changes.

## Accessibility and responsive standards

- Use semantic HTML and a logical heading hierarchy.
- Provide descriptive labels for controls, meaningful link text, and useful alternative text for informative images; use empty alt text for decorative images.
- Ensure the site is operable with a keyboard, has visible focus states, and uses accessible navigation and form labels.
- Maintain WCAG 2.2 AA contrast targets for text and controls where applicable.
- Support zoom and reflow without loss of content or functionality.
- Design and check layouts for mobile, tablet, and desktop widths.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; avoid motion that is not needed for understanding or operation.

## Project workflow

- Use Vite with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript unless the project owner approves a change.
- Keep GitHub as the source repository and configure the site for Render static-site deployment.
- Do not build pages beyond the currently approved milestone.
- Do not install unnecessary dependencies.
